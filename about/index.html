I live in Ireland and work on hardware and software for Formula Trinity, a student formula team. The rest of the time I climb, tinker with small computers, and write little programs that I need.

Email: me@mbdalpha.dev.

Things I spend time on:

  • Student racing. Mostly sensors, actuators, and the unglamorous bits that make a car move when someone asks it to.
  • Small tools. A browser page that lights a MoonBoard. Scrapers. Scripts. Things that should have existed already.
  • Computers I can hold. Embedded boards, NixOS boxes, whatever is currently half-working on the desk.
  • Climbing. Indoor walls, mostly. I like problems more than I like talking about them.
